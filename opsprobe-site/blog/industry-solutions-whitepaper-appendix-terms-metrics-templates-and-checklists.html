Glossary (operating terms)

  • Finding class: standardized category of resource waste or risk.
  • Risk lane: approval path tier based on impact and control sensitivity.
  • Closure proof: evidence object proving action execution and validation.
  • Recurrence rate: reappearance frequency of a finding class within fixed window.
  • Exception hygiene: proportion of exceptions closed or renewed before expiry.

KPI contract definitions

  • Closure lead time: median days from actionable finding creation to verified closure.
  • Realized-to-potential ratio: realized impact divided by potential estimate in same window.
  • Reassignment churn: average owner changes per finding before closure.
  • Retry inflation ratio: retry events over baseline runtime event volume.

All KPI calculations must include window, inclusion scope, exclusions, and version tag.

Templates

  • Evidence packet: summary, control map, execution proofs, retention manifest.
  • Weekly triage sheet: finding class, owner, due date, lane, status, blocker.
  • Executive one-pager: resolved classes, unresolved backlog, exception posture, next action.
  • Policy change note: what changed, why, compatibility impact, migration action.

Checklists

Rollout readiness checklist

  • Owner matrix defined for all in-scope finding classes.
  • Risk lanes documented with approver identities.
  • Evidence packet template approved by security and finance.
  • KPI semantics versioned and published.
  • Exception expiry policy activated.

Quarterly governance checklist

  • Recurrence trend reviewed by finding class.
  • Closure lead time variance reviewed by owner group.
  • Policy compatibility changes documented.
  • Residual risk register updated with named owners.
