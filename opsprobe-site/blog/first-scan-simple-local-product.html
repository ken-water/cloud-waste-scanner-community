How the product stays easy to use

The point of the rebuild is to keep the buying and setup experience simple while the scanner itself stays local and trustworthy.

1. Keep the public story simple

The home page, pricing page, download page, and support pages should help a user decide whether the product fits and how fast they can get value.

2. Make purchase friction low

Three clear offers are easier to understand than a long list of plans or edition names.

3. Make downloads easy to verify

Show the package name and checksum so a cautious team can validate the file before installing it.

4. Keep scanning local

The scanner should keep credentials and evidence on the customer machine so the trust boundary stays small.

5. The maintenance bar drops

  • One homepage.
  • One pricing page.
  • One download page.
  • One support path.
