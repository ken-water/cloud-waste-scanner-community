Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Cloud Waste Scanner ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our desktop application and website.

2. Data We Collect

  • Personal Information: We only collect your email address when you purchase a license. This is used solely for delivering your license key and important product updates.
  • Payment Information: All payments are processed by PayPal. We do not store your credit card details or PayPal login credentials.
  • Cloud Credentials: Your AWS, Azure, and GCP credentials (Access Keys, Service Accounts) are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. We never transmit these credentials to our servers.
  • Scan Data: The results of your cloud scans are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your infrastructure data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your email address to:

  • Send you your license key.
  • Verify your license status when you activate the app.
  • Notify you of critical security updates.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. Your license validation is performed using secure cryptographic signatures. Your cloud credentials never leave your machine.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ken@upp.ltd.
