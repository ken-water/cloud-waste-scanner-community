Security First Architecture

Your Credentials Never Leave Your Device.

We built Cloud Waste Scanner with a paranoid "Local-First" philosophy. We do not want your data, and our architecture guarantees we can't access it.

Zero-Knowledge Design

Local Storage Only

AWS Access Keys, Azure Service Principals, and GCP JSON keys are stored exclusively in an encrypted SQLite database on your local machine (`%APPDATA%`).

Direct API Calls

The app communicates directly from your IP address to AWS/Azure endpoints. No middleman servers, no proxying of sensitive data.

GDPR Compliant

Since we never collect your infrastructure data, we are compliant by design. Your scan results stay on your disk.

Security FAQ

Do you have access to my AWS account?

No. The access keys are stored locally on your machine. We (the developers of CWS) never see them. The app acts as a client-side browser for your infrastructure.

What permissions are required?

We recommend creating a Read-Only IAM user. CWS primarily uses `Describe*` and `List*` permissions. Write permissions are only needed if you use the "Cleanup" feature, and even then, you can restrict it to specific resource types.

Does it work behind a corporate firewall?

Yes. CWS Pro supports HTTP/HTTPS proxies, including those requiring authentication. You can configure this in the Settings > Network tab.
