Lightweight operations

No account database is required for first-contact feedback.

  • Feedback is collected through a static form endpoint.
  • No cloud account credentials are requested.
  • Operational context is enough: OS, version, provider, error, expected outcome.
  • For beta access issues, include your OS, app version, and download path.

What we track

We use server logs and submitted feedback to understand broken flows. We do not use this website to scan customer cloud accounts.
