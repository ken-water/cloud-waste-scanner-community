What you get

Four skills for the work after a scan.

CWS still performs the scan. Skills help translate the evidence into language and artifacts that humans can act on safely.

Report Explainer

Explain findings for operators, finance reviewers, and executives without inventing unsupported savings.

Weekly Brief

Turn current and prior evidence into a concise weekly cloud waste review.

Playbook Writer

Draft cleanup plans with pre-checks, rollback notes, risk level, and owner-ready next steps.

Export Auditor

Check whether CSV, JSON, TXT, or pasted evidence is complete enough to share.
